Hedera Agent Accounts give AI agents programmable, non-custodial spending accounts on Hedera. Set limits and approved merchants on-chain, and your private keys never leave your wallet. This new approach measures and prices each EVM opcode, precompile, and system contract based on actual computational demands — enabling decentralized applications to operate at scale on Hedera without sacrificing Ethereum compatibility or network security.
